Individual therapy

Regular one-to-one sessions for stress, anxiety, low mood, burnout, grief, relationships, or a feeling that something isn't right. Start with a short check-in so you can choose a professional whose focus matches what you're facing.

  • 50–60 minute video sessions
  • The same professional each time
  • Secure messages between sessions
  • Reminders before every session
Get started

Couples & family therapy

Sessions for partners or families, focused on the patterns between you rather than on any one person. Useful for communication, conflict, big transitions, or rebuilding trust.

  • Join together or from separate locations
  • Professionals trained in couples and family work
Get started

Group therapy

A small group led by a verified professional, meeting regularly around a shared experience — exam stress, workplace burnout, grief, or new parenthood. Members appear by first name or pseudonym only.

  • Always led by a licensed professional
  • Lower cost than one-to-one sessions
Ask about upcoming groups

Teen & youth counselling

Counselling for ages 12 to 17 with professionals experienced in working with young people. A parent or guardian gives consent and is kept appropriately involved, while the young person has their own space to talk.

  • Parent or guardian consent required
  • Clear agreement on what is shared with parents
