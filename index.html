Who we are

A calmer way to
care for your mind

Mindora exists on a simple idea — that getting support should feel ordinary, safe and unhurried. We are a small practice of registered therapists in Nairobi. We work with individuals, couples, families and teams, and we do it in whatever way suits you: in our rooms, or by video from wherever you are. No waiting lists you never hear back from, no being passed between people. You speak to someone, and then you keep speaking to them.

Simple process

Getting support,
made easy

Tell us what's going on

Send a short message, or call if that's easier. You don't need the right words for it — a sentence is enough to start.

We find the right person

We match you with a therapist whose experience fits what you're dealing with, and who you can see in the way that suits you.

Your first session

Meet your therapist in our rooms or by video. The first session is a chance to talk, ask questions and see whether it feels right.

Keep going at your pace

A regular slot with the same therapist, for as long as it helps. You decide when you're ready to stop.

  • Individual therapy Weekly one-to-one sessions with a registered therapist, in our Nairobi rooms or by video.

What to expect

Everything you need in
one steady place

Book a first session

Confidential

What you say stays between you and your therapist. The few legal exceptions are explained to you in your first session, before anything else.

Sessions that hold

A regular slot with the same therapist each week, so you're not re-explaining yourself to someone new.

In the room or online

Come to our rooms in Nairobi, or join by video. You can switch between the two whenever it suits you.

Not only for individuals

Couples, families, groups and workplace teams — the same practice, the same therapists.

A clear first step

One conversation before you commit to anything, so you know who you'd be seeing and what it would cost.

No pressure to continue

You decide how long you come for. Ending is a normal part of therapy, not a failure of it.
