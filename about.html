Mental health before crisis

Too often, support in Kenya arrives only after a breakdown. Mindora is a Kenyan digital platform built to change that: easy, confidential access to licensed professionals early, while things are still manageable.

Our mission, vision and motto

Our mission

To connect people across Kenya with trusted, licensed professionals, so they can get confidential support early — before struggles become crises.

Our vision

A Kenya where caring for your mind is as ordinary as caring for your body — and an Africa where no one waits for a crisis to get support.

Our motto

Mental health before crisis.

What Mindora is

A platform that connects you with independent, licensed counsellors and psychologists. We handle check-ins, booking, secure video, messaging and payments. The professionals provide the care.

How we verify professionals

Every professional uploads their practising licence, national ID and degree certificate. Our team reviews each document before the profile goes live, and any change to qualifications is reviewed again.

How we protect your data

We treat your information as sensitive personal data under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. You can download your data or ask us to delete your account from your settings at any time.
